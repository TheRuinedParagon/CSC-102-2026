My degree is in Digital Maker and Fabrication.
It specializes in using CAD and other tools to quickly prototype, evaluate, and bring creations to the market through these technologies.

The main objectives of this course are:

-Use digital and physical tools to engineer/build prototypes that tech patents can then be applied for;
-Be able to demonstrate the ability to evaluate material and build technique options during the creation of products and their respective prototypes;
-And to produce products that balance form and function while reflecting on current and future trends in design along with their human factors.


Classes that I need to take include courses such as DBM100 (3D Build Tools), TCH200 (Product Development), and RBT205 (Mechanisms and Materials). There are many others to do, but these are a few from the list.
